## How to sign

For your first pull request:

1. Comment on the PR: `I have read and agree to the CLA at
   https://github.com/Interfer0/CaptureSpike/blob/main/CLA.md`
2. Include your GitHub username, real name, and the date.

The project owner will record your agreement as a comment
on the PR. This comment is your signed CLA for this project.

For subsequent PRs from the same GitHub account, your
earlier agreement covers you — no need to re-sign.

## Why a CLA?

Without a CLA, contributors own copyright in their
contributions. The project owner cannot:
- Offer commercial licenses (would need permission from
  every contributor)
- Change the license in the future
- Sell the project (the buyer can't get a clean copyright
  assignment)

The CLA solves this without forcing the project to be
proprietary: the project stays GPL-3.0-or-later for the
community, and the owner can also offer a commercial
license to companies that prefer not to deal with GPL
obligations. Both versions can coexist.
